CC map

Any MIDI controller's CCs (USB-A) drive parameters, with Surface set to CC Only or beside a surface. A bound CC is taken from Move. On the device: Shift+Vol+Sample for learn, or Master FX Settings → CC Map.

Surface pages

The Custom layout on an E16 or EC4 (Global Settings → Surfaces → Surface Nav): pages of sixteen knobs, each driving any parameter. Saved with the set. On the device: Master FX Settings → Surface Layout, or hold a knob on the surface to learn.
